    Hi, I'm Yao Xiao (肖尧) 👋, currently a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), and a joint Ph.D. student in the Department of Data Science at City University of Hong Kong (CityUHK). I am advised by Prof. Tong Xu and Prof. Xiangyu Zhao.

    My research mainly focuses on Multimodal Learning, Large Language Models, and Mechanism Interpretability. I am interested in understanding and building reliable, interpretable, and adaptive AI systems.
