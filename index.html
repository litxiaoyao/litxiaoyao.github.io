About Me

Hi, I am Yao Xiao (肖尧), a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at the University of Science and Technology of China, advised by Prof. Tong Xu.

My research mainly focuses on large language models and multimodal learning, with an interest in building reliable, interpretable, and adaptive AI systems.
